Fruitfly Geometry · Q-05 · step 2

The low spike

Almost every attempt dies at the half-height spike at x = 972. This page shows what the fly's eye sees of it, and what each proposed fix changes, before any score is looked at.

Found while building this: F-02, expansion rate 4x too fast

flydash/fly.py computed closing speed as X_SPEED × FRAME_STEPS, but the cube moves X_SPEED × DT per physics step, so θ̇ (and with it the LC4 drive) was 4x too high for every object. Fixed and covered by a test. Size (θ) and the LPLC2 drive were right. Everything on this page uses the corrected numbers.

Drive as the spike approaches

Total looming drive (LC4 + LPLC2, Hz per neuron) against distance, approaching left to right. The dashed horizontal line is the drive at the moment the Giant Fiber fired for the tall spike on seed 1000. It is one seed at one moment, a rough guide and not a threshold: the brain integrates over time, so drive held for longer can fire at a lower level.

For you to decide

  1. Which option, and the reason in terms of the fly or the game, not the score. If it is the eye, what number, and where does it come from?
  2. Is the 12-unit spike the only low obstacle that matters? Many sit in pits between blocks, where a separate rule hides them: the fly only sees objects that overlap its own height.
  3. Which noise seeds are training and which are held out (Fly Status step 3). This has to be fixed before any tuning run.
